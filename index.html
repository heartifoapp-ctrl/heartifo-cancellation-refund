Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy explains how ride cancellations, applicable charges and refund requests are handled on Heartifo.

Last Updated: 18 September 2026
01

Ride Cancellation

Customers may cancel a ride through the Heartifo App. Cancellation may be available before or after a Captain has been assigned, subject to the applicable cancellation rules displayed in the App.

02

Cancellation Charges

A cancellation charge may apply in certain situations, including cancellation after a Captain has accepted the ride or after the Captain has started travelling towards the pickup location.

Important: Any applicable cancellation charge will be shown or communicated through the App where applicable.
03

Captain Cancellation

If a Captain cancels a ride, customers will generally not be charged a cancellation fee for that Captain cancellation. Heartifo may help the customer find another available Captain.

04

Refund Eligibility

Where a customer has been incorrectly charged or is otherwise eligible for a refund, Heartifo may review the transaction and process an appropriate refund in accordance with the applicable payment method and circumstances.

05

Payment Issues

If your payment was deducted but the ride was not successfully completed or your transaction appears incorrect, please contact Heartifo Support with the relevant ride or transaction details.

06

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed through the applicable payment provider or original payment method where possible. The time required for the refund to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

07

Fraudulent or Abusive Requests

Heartifo may review repeated, fraudulent or abusive refund requests. Accounts involved in payment abuse or fraudulent activity may be restricted in accordance with applicable law.

08

How to Request Help

If you believe you were charged incorrectly or need assistance with a cancellation or refund, contact Heartifo Support and provide your registered mobile number and relevant ride details.
